# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file.

The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-17

### Added

- `TheseusSettings.glassStyle` (`automatic`, `iOS26`, `iOS27`) and `effectiveGlassStyle`: `automatic` draws iOS 26's material on iOS 26 and iOS 27's everywhere else. iOS 26.5's regular glass mixes toward white at 0.687 (Clear) and 0.846 toward 0.974 (Tinted) in light mode and is a plain mix toward 0.149 at 0.764 (Clear) and toward 0.096 at 0.78 (Tinted) in dark mode; its clear glass is 1.049 x the backdrop + 19, where iOS 27's is 0.95 x + 31.
- `TheseusSettings.followsSystemTint` (on by default) and `systemTintSpectrum`: `tintSpectrum` follows the system's Liquid Glass setting, iOS 27's Tint Amount (`UIViewGlassTintAmount` in the global defaults, 0.5 until the user moves it) or iOS 26's Clear and Tinted choice (`UIViewGlassLegibilitySetting`), read at launch and whenever it changes. Setting `tintSpectrum` yourself or enabling its persistence turns following off.
- Demo: the settings screen follows the system setting, picks the glass style and offers iOS 26 and iOS 27 in the version override.
- `TheseusLensProfile.shadeCapOnsetShare`, `shadeTopExponent`, `shadeTopLobeShift` and `shadeSideExponent` shape the two-lobe shade, and `capOutlineOnly` keeps the superellipse to the outline (coverage, contour and rim line) while the shade and refraction follow the stadium.
- `TheseusTabBar.nativeUnselectedTint`, the new default of `unselectedTintColor`: unselected items read the glass behind them as native's do, each channel 0.317 of the glass less 0.254 in light appearance (a near-black that takes the page's hue) and white in dark. Any other colour is used as given.
- Demo: `-autodrag 1` drives every README preview scene from inside the app: the hero, settings and fallback lenses follow a scripted path, and the switch, slider and tab bar scenes play scripted touches through the controls' own press, move and release paths (`@_spi(Scripting)` entry points), so the recordings need no hand on the simulator and move at a steady 60 Hz.
- Demo: `-rigCycle RRGGBB,RRGGBB` with `-rigCyclePeriod <s>` alternates the tab bar page's flat colour, so the bars' adaptation can be recorded. A `TheseusDemoUITests` UI testing bundle presses and drags the switches, sliders and tab bars (native and Theseus, both appearances), which reaches simulators the Xcode device tools cannot, such as iOS 26.5.
- Demo: `-rigTintHex RRGGBB` and `-rigTintAlpha` pick the tinted pills' colour and alpha in the `native27` rig, and `-rigBackdrop hstripes:<pt>` or `vstripes:<pt>` draws grey stripes of that period behind its pills. `-rigKinds` picks which rows it shows (`0` regular, `1` tinted, `2` clear, so `02` drops the tinted row), `-rigPillWidth` and `-rigPillHeight` size the pills and `-rigPadding` sets the Theseus pills' padding.
- `TheseusShape.continuousCapsule` (on by default): a capsule draws native's continuous ends. The edge leaves the straight side half a radius before the cap, runs up to 1.7 % of the radius inside it just past where the cap begins and rejoins the circle 0.6 radii in, a table read off `UIGlassEffect` pills of four radii that agree in units of the radius (0.03 px rms; a single Gaussian managed 0.12). The native rig pill, the tab bar's chrome and its pill trace native's outlines where ours ran up to 1.9 px outside.
- Demo: `-rigBackdrop ramp` (a grey ramp across the native pills) and `xcoord` or `ycoord` (each pixel's coordinate in red and green) in the `native27` rig.

- `TheseusLensProfile.highlightInLinearLight` (default `true`; the knob glass sets `false`): the rim line adds its strength in linear light, as the native tab bar lens does (+70 levels over a dark page, +23 to +35 over mid grey, +9 near white in dark).
- Demo: a `gsteps` rig backdrop (`-rigPattern gsteps -rigStripe 12`), vertical bands of eight greys from 0x10 to 0xF0, so one parked lens reads its rim against several backdrop levels at once.
- Demo: pixel-exact rig backdrops (`-rigPattern coord`, `xcoord`, `ycoord`, `checker`, `vstripes6`, `hstripes6`; `coord` writes each pixel's x and y into red and green, so a refracted pixel decodes to where it was sampled), and `-tabIcon bars`, a striped test icon on every item of both bars, so the lens's warp and colour split of an item can be read side by side.

- `TheseusView.providesCaptureSnapshot`: a glass view keeps a copy of each rendered frame, and other glass views capturing the tree draw that copy in place of a flat material veil. The tab bar's chrome and pill turn it on, so the drag lens refracts the frosted chrome as rendered; before, it saw the sharp backdrop through a translucent veil.
- `TheseusRefraction.rimDispersion` and `rimDispersionDepth`: a red-to-blue split along the lens rim, red leading clockwise, fading over a depth from the edge (a third as deep on the sides). `TheseusConfiguration.lensShadowOpacity`, `lensShadowOffset` and `lensShadowRadius`: a lens drop shadow as a Gaussian ring around the outline moved down (0 keeps the old 2 % downward glow).
- `TheseusLensMaterial.magnification`, `interiorDetail`, `interiorAverage`, `rimDispersion`, `rimDispersionDepth`, `shadowOpacity`, `shadowOffset` and `shadowRadius`, with Lens Lab knobs for each.
- Demo: grey stripe backdrops (`-rigPattern gvstripes` or `ghstripes`) on the tab bar page and in the Lens Lab, and the tab bar page now honours the Lens Lab's `lens.*` knobs (read at launch).

- `TheseusRefraction.interiorAverage`: the width in points of the backdrop a surface averages before its body map, read from the blurred texture's mip chain in place of the blur alone (0 keeps the blur). The tab bar chrome and pill use 80 pt.
- iOS 27.0 material model, fitted next to native `UIGlassEffect` views over light and dark backdrops in both appearances: regular glass is a body mix toward an appearance pole (`neutralBodyScale`), clear glass and bar chrome are an additive lift (`TheseusTheme.luminanceLift`, new), and the clear-to-tinted spectrum scales both.
- Tab bar drag lens rebuilt from measurements of the native lens: the backdrop keeps its scale, a bevel band displaces it outward along a linear rise, a plateau and a tail, a two-pixel contour marks the rim with a pixel-thin dispersed highlight inside it (strong at the top, weaker at the bottom, faint on the sides, warm outside and cool inside), the band and the interior below the top and side rims carry a soft shading, and the hovered item grows as native does (label 1.22x, glyph 1.62x, about the glyph's baseline). `TheseusLensMaterial` (band width, depth, profile, dispersion, blur, per-appearance lift, item and icon scale, motion response) is public on `TheseusTabBar.lensMaterial` and applies live; `TheseusRefraction.lensProfile` carries the bevel shape and the rim lines.
- `TheseusLensMaterial.poleMix`/`poleMixDark` (luminance mix toward white that keeps the chroma) and `TheseusLensProfile.contourSide`, `shadeSide`, `shadeSideDark`, `shadeOnset`, `shadeOnsetDark`, `shadeSideOnset`, `highlightBottomDark`; `TheseusSwitch.glassMaterial` and `TheseusSlider.glassMaterial` (default `.knobGlass`), `TheseusSwitch.Layout.glassOverrun`/`glassOverrunResistance`, `TheseusSlider.Layout.glassSize`.
- Demo: a Lens Lab screen (`-demo lenslab`) with the native bar over the Theseus bar, every lens knob as a slider backed by `UserDefaults` (`lens.<knob>`, writable from outside the app while it runs), and a grid of static lenses that renders one tile per value of `-labKnob`/`-labValues`; band (`-rigPattern hstripes -rigStripe 12 -rigPhase`) and flat (`flat:RRGGBB`) backdrops for the tab bar demo and the lab.
- `TheseusTheme.backdropGain`: multiplier on the backdrop between the body and the lift.
- `TheseusConfiguration.lensFill`, `lensFillColor` and `lensRimScale` (the body mixed toward a colour and the rim's displacement, frost, shade, contour and line scaled on their own: the knob-to-lens morphs of the switch and slider, and the pill-to-lens morph of the tab bar), `TheseusView.onPresent` (called once a rendered frame has completed) and `TheseusView.renderNow()`.
- Demo: `-preview native27` puts native `UIGlassEffect` regular, tinted and clear pills next to the Theseus presets on the same gradient (`-rigBackdrop dark` for a dark backdrop), the rig behind these numbers.
- VoiceOver support: `TheseusSwitch` reports On/Off and toggles on activate, `TheseusSlider` is adjustable with increment/decrement, `TheseusTabBar` exposes items as buttons with the selected trait and the `.tabBar` container trait.
- Reduce Motion: springs, fly-overs and lens morphs collapse to instant moves with short cross-fades in all three controls.
- `TheseusSlider.setValue(_:animated:)` honours `animated`.
- `TheseusConfiguration.tint(_:intensity:)`: per-view glass tint mirroring the native `Glass.tint(_:)` API, applied on both the Metal and fallback backends.
- `TheseusConfiguration.regular`, `.clear` and `.lens` presets.
- `TheseusSettings.tintSpectrum`: global clear-to-tinted spectrum replicating the iOS 27 "Tint Amount" slider on iOS 13+, with `enableTintSpectrumPersistence(in:key:)` and `disableTintSpectrumPersistence()` for UserDefaults persistence.
- Shape merging: `TheseusMergedShape`, `TheseusShape.mergedShapes` and `mergeSmoothing` (smooth-union SDF, Metal backend only).
- `TheseusTabBar.glassTintIntensity` and `preferredItemWidth`.
- OKLab highlight tinting: `TheseusTheme.highlightTintPreservation`, `surfaceTintWeight`, `edgeTintWeight`.
- `TheseusTheme.backdropSaturation` and `neutralBodyScale`.
- Refraction knobs: `thickness`, `magnification`, `magnificationCenterY`, `interiorSharpness`, `edgeIridescence`.
- Capture compensation: effect views and Metal-backed glass capture correctly under `layer.render(in:)`; template-tinted images capture at full strength; `TheseusView.setCaptureExcludedViews(_:)`.
- Per-link display cadence: `DisplayLinkDriver` runs at the highest active rate and delivers to each subscriber at its own rate, ordered by `CallbackPriority`.
- Glass tint desaturates under `tintAdjustmentMode` dimming (presented sheets) on both backends.

### Changed

- `TheseusTabBar` draws iOS 26.5's bar under `glassStyle` `iOS26`, read from flat sweeps behind the native bar (23 to 33 greys and 10 colours per setting and appearance).
  - Adaptive: the bar picks light or dark glass from the page behind it, as native's does. In light appearance it turns dark below a luminance of 53/255 and back above 125/255; in dark appearance it turns light above 221/255 and back below 148/255. iOS 27's bar keeps the appearance.
  - Adaptation: in Clear, native's body follows an adapted luminance, which a new page first reaches through its old setting (the page's change at a slope of 0.27 in light, 0.32 in dark); Tinted shows the new page at once. The setting, and the style when it flips, then move on a critically damped spring of 13 rad/s after a sampling delay. Theseus runs the same spring 0.08 s after the chrome's next capture; recorded flips and plain changes track native's within 40 ms. `TheseusTabBar.invalidateBackground()` tells the bar the page behind it changed.
  - Chrome: a measured luminance curve per style and setting, with a chroma scale that moves with the luminance (`TheseusTheme` body curves, 16 knots, drawn by the shader and the stand-in veil alike). It lands within 3 levels of native over 20 backdrops, where the earlier mix ran up to 80 off once the native bar had flipped.
  - Pill: the chrome clamped, then 1.126 x - 0.197 at saturation 1.065 in light and 0.956 x + 0.136 at 1.169 in dark, the same in Clear and Tinted. It is flat, with no top shade, and 74 x 53.5 pt. Items sit 61.5 pt apart on a 328 pt bar.
  - Items: the selected item is its tint plus half the pill behind it (less half of white in light), and the unselected ones take the chrome's chroma at a luminance of 0.1 x the chrome's (light) or 0.951 + 0.045 x (dark). iOS 26's own systemBlue (0088FF light, 0091FF dark) comes through as is. All read within 2 levels of native.
  - Shadow: a blur of sigma 16.8 pt dropped 8.8 pt under light glass at 0.18 easing to 0.13 near white in Clear (halving past a luminance of 237/255) and 0.1 in Tinted, and of sigma 13 pt dropped 13 pt at 0.03 under dark glass, within a level of native along its whole profile.
  - Rim: iOS 26's diagonal key and fill lights (upper left and lower right) in a ring about 4 px deep inside the outline, mixing toward its vibrancy matrix over the chrome's colour. The body reaches the outline, without iOS 27's dark outer line.
- `TheseusTabBar` on iOS 27: the chrome and pill use the same measured curves, fitted on 16 greys and 6 colours per appearance with the tint amount's response kept from the material model. The dark chrome was up to 25 levels off over colours and is now within a level; the pill was up to 13 off and is now within 1.3. The bar's shadow never drew, since `CALayer.render(in:)` leaves out a shadow path; it is now an exact Gaussian image, sigma 2.8 pt dropped 8 pt at 0.012 in both appearances, native's glass shadow for its height.
- Native glass now copies iOS 27.0's own formulas where they could be read, from the private DesignLibrary framework that builds the glass recipe and from the IR of QuartzCore's shader library.
  - Tint: `GlassRim.tintMatrix` is the system's tint matrix. A tint colour T with hue h, saturation s and lightness l maps the glass's luma Y to a ramp between two colours in encoded values. Light appearance runs from HSL(h, 1.2s, 0.6l) to T as Y goes from 0.15 to 1; dark appearance runs from T to HSL(h, 0.95s, 1.1l) as Y goes from 0.15 to 0.75. Greys scale each channel instead (a 0.75 fill of the grey, a burn toward l + 0.8(1 - l), a dodge by 0.2l). It matches the system's matrices for 55 colours in both appearances within its own 1e-4 rounding, and the tinted body now reads within a level of native. The tint keeps up to 1.2 of headroom and goes as low as -0.75 before it mixes in, as the system's filter does.
  - Highlight: the line and glow are native's key and fill lights. Each fades over its depth (1 pt for the line with a 0.75 curvature, 8 pt for the glow), weighs the normal against 80 deg and 52 deg spreads, and the glow is remapped by t/(1 + 12.33(1 - t)). The normal is ovalised toward the position over the half size, as native's SDF is.
  - Layering: the tint and the highlight apply to the composited pixel, glass and band blended at the edge first, in native's layer order.
  - Refraction: native glass refracts along the circular capsule's normal ovalised by the material (0.5 regular, none clear), measures the bevel depth from the continuous ends and blurs each sample at its own depth. The outward sample is taken raw at full blur. Clear glass over horizontal stripes went from 15 to 7 levels at the 99th percentile inside, and its cap now lands within a pixel of native.
  - Face: the luminance cap keeps native's chroma law, the chroma scaled by t*(1 + 0.3(1 - t)) with t the capped share.
  - Band: native's pair of horizontal key and fill lights reaching 0.5333 pt past the frame, weighted by the ovalised normal against the spread (106 deg light, 75 deg dark and clear) and remapped by t/(1 + k(1 - t)) with k = 1/amount - 2. It moves the sharp backdrop toward the darker of it and its own face, then shades the pixel by x*(1 + bias*w*(3 - 2x)). The same remap gives the highlight line k = 0 and the glow 11.33.
  - Shape: native glass measures every distance with QuartzCore's own continuous-corner function (`supercircle_sdf`) in place of the capsule-end table, and the band reads the backdrop as native's half-resolution capture does, averaged in linear light, adding back full-resolution detail where the band is weaker. Single stripe-edge pixels at the tip went from 62 levels off to 28.
  - iOS 26: under `glassStyle` `iOS26` native glass switches to iOS 26.5's recipe, read from its layers: its face (saturation 1 Clear to 2 Tinted, a white fill of 0.4 to 0.8 in light and a black one of 0.4 to 0.6 in dark, with the luminance map measured over greys and colours), no band and no ring shadow, a diagonal key and fill highlight (-45 deg and 135 deg, 90 deg spread, curvature 0.7, no glow) through iOS 26's vibrancy matrix (light 0.1 x 15 saturation + 0.9, dark 1.35 x 2.22 + 0.15), refraction of 0.8H over H/4 (clear H over 0.36H, at most 60 and 20 pt), an outer sample at 0.3 over the outer two thirds of a point, a blur of 1 radius share deep and 0.5 at the rim with no fill, the shadow below, and the tint left at the frame. Over 16 iOS 26.5 captures the rim went from 14 to 32 levels on average to under 2, and the inside to within a level; pill centres over eight greys and colours sit within a level of native in both appearances, where the earlier plain mix was up to 58 off on colours.
  - Shadow: the drop shadow's strength follows the glass's short side, as its blur does. iOS 27 ramps it from 0.012 to 0.12 (light) or 0.18 (dark) between 64 and 160 pt, on the same scale as the blur radius. iOS 26.5 blurs every size by 16.8 pt and draws 0.07 up to 64 pt; past that light holds near 0.105 and eases to 0.09 while dark climbs from 0.042 to 0.155. Neither draws one under clear glass. The shadow uses an exact error function cut off two standard deviations out, and the drawable grows until the shadow fits, so large glass and iOS 26 glass no longer clip it at the 17 pt padding. Over 11 sizes from 30 to 180 pt, in both appearances on both systems, every pixel of the shadow below and above the glass lands within a level of native, where it was up to 27 levels off.
- `TheseusConfiguration.regular`, `.clear` and their tinted forms draw `UIGlassEffect` as iOS 27.0 builds it, read from its own Core Animation filters and fitted over flat greys, stripes and colours in both appearances (`TheseusConfiguration.GlassRim`).
  - Refraction: the backdrop is pulled inward by half the short side over a quarter of it (clear glass 0.65 over 0.36), at most 60 and 20 pt; regular glass also mixes an outward pull into its outer point, 0.6 of it at the frame.
  - Frost: a blur whose radius is set at the point the glass reads (sigma up to 9.6 pt in regular glass, 1.3 pt in clear), then a 14.6 pt fill that lightens what lies under it in light appearance and darkens it in dark before covering it, both averaging encoded values as native does. ### Fixed

- The first press on the switch or the slider could show a frosted rectangle with a black dot for two frames on iOS 26: the glass drew its first frame before taking its new size.
- A glass view whose size times the screen scale came out a hair under a whole pixel (59.333 pt is 177.999 px) got a drawable a pixel small, which the compositor stretched: the knob glass drew 1 px small and half a pixel soft at every edge. Drawable sizes round to the nearest pixel.
- Captures whose region was a whole number of pixels plus a floating-point hair allocated a pixel more than the region, so what the glass refracted was resampled by 196/197.
- The tab bar's edge images (its contour and glow) were drawn a pixel taller than their layer for the same reason, which squeezed the bar's bottom edge up a pixel.
- A fully transparent `nearColor` or `farColor` (the way to turn the edge highlight off) drew a dark crescent where the highlight would have been.
- A round or rounded glass view showed a second dark ring about 8 pt inside its rim. The inner vignette was baked into the blurred backdrop, which the bevel refraction then displaced inward; it is drawn in screen space again, inside the rim where it was.
- A capture whose region reaches past its source view or the screen spreads the edge pixels over the rest, so glass near that edge reads the edge colour where it read transparent black (a dark crescent with a cyan fringe at the end of a pill near the edge).

- A glass view under a scale transform (the lens morphs of the tab bar, switch and slider) captured its padding unscaled, so everything seen through it drew up to 6 % small while it grew or shrank; the landed tab item read 95 % of its size 200 ms after a release.
- `blur.radius` was applied in render pixels instead of points, so every surface blurred at a third of its configured radius on a 3x screen (the tab bar's 18 pt read as sigma 5 px against native's 17 px).
- The flat stand-in that `layer.render` captures use for a Metal-backed surface mixed toward white where the shader adds, so a lens over the bar saw a darker bar than the live one; the stand-in now linearises the body transfer around the captured backdrop's mean luminance.
- A tinted surface composited its tint over the raw backdrop instead of the processed body.

- On iOS 26 and later a horizontal drag on a slider, switch or tab bar inside a navigation stack started the interactive pop; the controls now refuse foreign pan recognizers like UIKit's own.
- `enableTintSpectrumPersistence` missed writes from other processes and other `UserDefaults` instances; it now observes the key through KVO.
- Settings, power and thermal notifications reached UIKit off the main thread.
- `TheseusSlider` never tore down its keep-alive lens when leaving the window.
- The renderer could stop rendering for good after three early returns left the frame counter at the in-flight cap; each renderer now owns its completion event.
- Thin glass shapes could draw a NaN line through their centre (zero-length edge gradient).
- `TheseusCapability.iosMajorVersion` was capped at 18.
- Per-tick morph updates rebuilt the blur kernel every frame.
- Self-capture feedback loop in the `.layerRendering` capture path saturated tinted glass and the `.clear` preset to a solid colour.
- Blend pipelines squared the drawable alpha, clipping any `opacity < 1` glass to white over bright backdrops.
- Capture region did not match the drawable, shrinking and offsetting everything seen through the glass.
- Inner-shadow pass traced only the primary shape, leaving a crease through merged shapes.
- Lens sampled its destination content mid-animation; capture lag ghosted a slot-shifted duplicate during fast drags.
- App-active auto-resume restarted hidden kept-alive lenses; tab-bar hide race and missing lens teardown on window loss.
- `TheseusCapability.isUnsupportedDevice` flagged every device (prefix match stripped the comma).
- Fallback neutral body ignored dark-mode trait changes.
- `.tint(_, intensity: 0)` rendered as a third state instead of untinted glass.
- `blur.radius` was a no-op on non-lens surfaces.
- Template-tinted `UIImageView`s captured at a fraction of their strength through the glass.
- Drag jitter: quality-tier re-capture rate, unstable callback order, noisy acceleration estimate, premature follow settle, and the drag's acceleration stretch bypassing `stretchLimit`.
- Tab bar drag lens: the lens frame was derived from the hovered item's transformed frame, so it jumped by 25 % whenever the finger crossed an item centre; the item under the lens was never magnified when a drag began on the selected item; the item snapped back to 1x the instant the finger lifted; the release selected the item nearest the finger instead of the one under the lens; and the bevel rise was steeper than one pixel per pixel, folding neighbouring glyphs into mirrored slivers at the rim.
- Tab bar drag lens: the rim highlight width and fade were scaled by the screen scale (6 px wide at 3x instead of 2) and the band was half-blurred, where the native band is crisp.
- Capture veil (`layer.render` stand-in for Metal glass): a lifted dark bar's veil saturated at white and lost its lift, so the lens showed the bar 15 levels darker than it was.
- Item views are laid out through `bounds` and `center`, so a scale transform no longer feeds back into their frames.
